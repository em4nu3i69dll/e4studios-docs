# Support

Email [contacto@em4nu3l69dll.dev](mailto:contacto@em4nu3l69dll.dev) or join [E4Studios on Discord](https://discord.e4studios.xyz/).

## Include

- Resource name and version.
- Framework and relevant dependencies.
- Expected and actual behavior.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- Full error messages and useful screenshots.
- Tebex transaction reference for purchase enquiries.

## Remove private data

Review logs before sending them. Do not share passwords, tokens, license keys, database credentials or player personal data. Do not publish a complete `server.cfg` file.

Remote access and passwords are not an initial requirement for support.

## Scope

Support concerns documented resource behavior. Each product page sets its specific conditions. Custom development and external integrations require a separate agreement.

## Payments and privacy

Payment issues may be raised with [Tebex](https://www.tebex.io/contact/checkout). Use our public email address for personal data requests.
